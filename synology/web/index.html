Per-file download method

Single connection avoids splitting, merging, and full-file verification to reduce NAS disk activity after a download.

Single-connection trade-off

The final byte size and transfer errors are checked, but SHA-256 and ZIP integrity verification are skipped. Corruption detection is weaker.
